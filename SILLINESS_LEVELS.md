HOW SILLINESS IS MEASURED

The scale (1-12/10):
  • 6/10: Something feels off
  • 7/10: You're starting to question
  • 8/10: Reality has minor physics violations
  • 10/10: Sanity is a suggestion
  • 12/10: Mathematically impossible (somehow works)
  • BEYOND: The scale broke. We're all on borrowed time.

Indicators of silliness progression:
  - Frequency of "wait, what?" thoughts
  - Number of times you check if you're dreaming
  - Realizing you can't remember how you got here
  - Laughter without knowing why
  - Existential questions about browsers/terminals
  - The urge to show someone else (no one believes you)
